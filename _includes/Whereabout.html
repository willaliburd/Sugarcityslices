Our Bakes

Sugar City Slices is a tiny, from scratch, home based cottage bakery in Bloomington, Indiana. We use no ultra-processed foods in our baking, only all-natural ingredients. We use locally sourced and organic ingredients whenever possible.

We offer pastries on a rotating basis including our Sugar City Slices with raisin or coconut filling, a variety of gluten-free, dairy-free treats, vegan hand pies, beef patties, scones, blondies, and various cakes.
